PART 1 - WEBSITE PRIV ACY NOTICE
1. Who We Are And What We Offer
Cybersecurity, ﬁnancial consultancy and related professional services. This Privacy Notice should explain how we 
collect, use, disclose, retain and protect personal information.When one visits www.datashield.it.com,one must get to 
contact us, request a quotation, subscribe to communications or otherwise interact with us through the website.
2. Information We May Collect
Depending on how one interacts with us, we may collect the name, job title, organisation, email address, telephone 
number, information included in enquiries or uploaded documents, quotation/service interests, correspondence 
records, technical information such as IP address and browser/device information, and cookie or analytics information 
where applicable.
3. How We Collect Information
We may collect information directly from the client when they complete a form, send an email, telephone us, request 
a quotation or provide information during an enquiry. Some technical information may be collected automatically 
through the website, server logs and permitted cookies or similar technologies. We may also receive business contact 
information from authorised representatives of organisations that engage with us.
4. Legal and Compliance Basis
We will process personal information only where there is an appropriate legal basis under the laws applicable to the 
particular processing activity. Depending on the circumstances, this may include consent, contractual necessity, 
compliance with a legal obligation, legitimate business interests, or another basis recognised by applicable data-
protection law. The precise basis should be conﬁrmed for each material processing activity before this notice is 
published.
5. Sharing of Information
We may share information with authorised personnel, professional advisers, technology/hosting providers, email or 
CRM providers, payment or accounting service providers, regulators, courts or public authorities where required, and 
other processors or service providers that support legitimate business operations. Appropriate contractual and security 
safeguards should be used where required.
6. International Transfers
If personal information is transferred or accessed outside the country in which it was collected, we will take steps 
required by applicable law to ensure an appropriate level of protection. The ﬁnal website notice should identify any 
material cross-border hosting or service-provider arrangements once conﬁrmed.
7. Retention
We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected 
and to meet legal, regulatory, contractual, accounting, dispute-resolution and security requirements. Speciﬁc retention 
periods should be documented in the company's retention schedule.
8. Security
We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against 
unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.Users should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive 
information through general website forms.

PART 4 - PROFESSIONAL SERVICES DISCLAIMER
Information published on this website is general in nature and is provided to describe Data Shield Advisory and its 
services. It should not be treated as a substitute for advice based on a client's particular circumstances. 
 
Data-protection and cybersecurity requirements should depend on the organisation, information processed, systems 
used, contractual arrangements and applicable law. Financial consultancy content on the website should not be 
interpreted as regulated investment, legal, tax or accounting advice unless the company is speciﬁcally authorised and 
engaged to provide the relevant service. 
 
No professional-client relationship is created merely by visiting the website, submitting a general enquiry or receiving 
preliminary information. A formal engagement begins only when accepted by the company under agreed terms. 
 
Regulatory fees, statutory charges or third-party costs displayed or discussed on the website should be clearly 
distinguished from the company's own professional fees and should be veriﬁed before a client commits to a service.
